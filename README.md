# 🎬Barbie as the Island Princess

<img width="265" height="376" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/84354828-e2fc-45a5-b72c-bc211e3e9765" />

 

*"Joy's not found in things such as necklaces and rings, but in love that's always there: that's the jewel beyond compare."*  - Princess Luciana

 

*Barbie as the Island Princess is a 2007 CGI animated musical film—the eleventh entry in the Barbie movie franchise—about Rosella ("Ro"), a shipwrecked girl raised by talking animals on a tropical island who travels to a royal kingdom and uncovers both her true heritage and a treacherous plot to steal the throne.*

 

# ❤️Why I love It

*  *The Nostalgia:* 2000s Barbie Golden Era and Memorable Soundtrack

*  *The Characters:* Rosella ("Ro"), Prince Antonio, Sagi, Azul & Tika, Queen Ariana, Princess Luciana and King Peter, Queen Danielle & Queen Marissa

*   *The Upside Down:* Turning Ro's World Upside Down. As noted in the film's official IMDb synopsis, Ro sees her peaceful island world "turn upside down" when Prince Antonio takes her and her animal friends into royal civilization—forcing a girl who climbs trees and talks to animals to adapt to ballgowns, tea parties, and palace intrigue.
